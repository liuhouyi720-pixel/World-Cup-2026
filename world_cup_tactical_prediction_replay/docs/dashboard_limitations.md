# Limitations

- Current 2026 event-level xT/xG data is not available in the project; historical national-team profiles are proxies.
- Teams, coaches, players, lineups, injuries, pressing, defensive structure, set pieces, and goalkeeper effects are incomplete or absent.
- Formation matchup data is largely historical and may have small or non-national samples.
- Clustering describes spatial similarity, not quality, causality, or a definitive tactical identity.
- Component weights and probability transforms are transparent demonstration choices, not optimized parameters.
- Strength falls back to a neutral mean; teams with sparse data naturally receive similar probabilities.
- Published match scores may include extra time. The result cache retains FT/AET/Pens status, while the displayed 1X2 probabilities are explicitly 90-minute estimates; this limits direct knockout score evaluation.
- Deterministic full simulation uses a fixed seeded bracket after qualification rather than recreating every official best-third routing permutation.
- The project is not intended for betting or claims of scientific forecasting accuracy.
